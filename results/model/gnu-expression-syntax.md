# GNU conditional, typeof and choose expression construction

The compiler model now constructs complete Cabs for GNU omitted-middle
conditionals, expression/type operands of all three `typeof` spellings, and
`__builtin_choose_expr`. These are general compiler-action constructors;
neither a specification parser nor a returned reference AST enters the model.
The existing C compiler, code generation, evaluation policy, C11 semantics,
Supported/Fits scope and captured sum inputs are unchanged.

`Gen5.expr_cond` already retains the distinct two-child `.condGNU` production.
Its Cabs is `CabsEcondGNU`, without copying its condition into a second syntax
child or reevaluating it. The independent grammar previously recognized only
the text lexer's combined `QUESTION_COLON`; phase four correctly retains two
preprocessing tokens. The grammar now also accepts `QUESTION COLON` under its
GNU setting. Both token occurrences, spellings, coordinates and recursive
physical origins remain separate. This also permits intervening whitespace
and comments; no token merging or location erasure manufactures agreement.

`Gen3.parse_btype` attaches a `.typeofSpec` action around the existing
`parse_expr_type` call, before CType/storage/alignment merging. Its actual
type-name branch wraps the already parsed specifiers/abstract declarator in
`.type`; its expression branch retains the actual expression action. The
builder selects `TSpec_typeof_type` or `TSpec_typeof_expr` from those typed
children, preserving qualifiers, declarators, nested syntax and source ranges.
It never reconstructs spelling from CType. The original compiler path alone
evaluates VLA bounds: there is no syntax replay of either branch.

`Gen4.unary` distinguishes `.choose` from the remaining builtins. The original
constant-expression parser and both expression branches supply its three
children. Both chosen and unchosen branches survive compiler value-stack pops;
the compiler's original `nocode_wanted` transitions still control execution.
The builder retains `GNUbuiltin_choose_expr` with every child, including its
unfolded constant condition and all unselected syntax. The real parser still
checks unselected operands for undeclared names and invalid operations.

Nesting inside unselected `_Generic` associations uses the existing
scanner-owned saved-word maps and `genericAssociationSyntax` snapshot wrapper.
The adopted `associationReplayErasure` theorem is reused unchanged. There is
no third replay wrapper, parser substitution or new parser-success premise.
The shared `unevaluated_syntax_cmp` harness serves the assertion, generic and
GNU suites; its three scopes stay distinct.

## Universal duties and remaining whole-C obligations

This adds no trusted premise and proves no construction theorem from finite
agreement. The eleven existing ghost contracts remain open. The new induction
cases in `RawConstruction_Statement`, `ParserAgreement_Statement` and
`RawParserAgreement_Statement` must prove: actual scoped typedef disambiguation;
complete children for both choose operands; conditional syntax with the
omitted operand distinct; VLA/type/expression child coverage in every enclosing
declarator and unevaluated context; and saved-word transport through arbitrary
nested replay. The shared local replay-erasure theorem supplies snapshot
restoration only. Whole-run `Erasure_Statement`/`RawErasure_Statement`, physical
transform/read correspondence, injected authority transport/erasure and
normalization agreement remain universal obligations.

The independent F1 grammar still owes its general text/phase-seven connection,
including the separate question/colon production, and Cabs/Ail/Core meaning
transport for these GNU forms. TypeAlignmentRepr, preprocessor correctness,
resource/fuel adequacy, compiler-model/binary refinement, Layer B's expression
simulation (including conditional single evaluation and VLA allocation),
runtime-library bodies and full per-TU construction remain open. CSem stays
C11 and the whole-compiler theorem has no refusal disjunct or new exclusion.

The original 56 inputs remain unchanged. Its remaining nonsuccesses include
two malformed inputs, K&R definitions, unused deferred inline functions,
local-label declarations and pragma-pack raw semantics. General declaration
and bitfield constraints, trailing member attributes and other whole-C gaps
remain separate lead tasks. Historical corpus counts are not pooled with
this lane, and sum cuts are never regenerated.
